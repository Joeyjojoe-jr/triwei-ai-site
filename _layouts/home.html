Read the original work—not an AI synopsis

TriWei links to the original author, organization, repository, or public institution. It does not publish AI-written summaries of third-party pieces. Original TriWei analysis will appear only after human research, authorship, source review, and approval.

Publication rule
  • Preserve the source's original title.
  • Link directly to the canonical source page, not a search or news intermediary.
  • Show the named author when the source supplies one; otherwise show the responsible publisher or institution without guessing.
  • Keep automated discovery separate from human-authored TriWei analysis.
